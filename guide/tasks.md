# Tasks and goals

The Tasks page shows every piece of work in your organization, from the moment you ask for it until you accept it. It has two views. The **Board** shows each task in a column by where it stands. **Goals** shows what the work is for: your measurable goals and the projects that move them forward.

## What you'll see

At the top are the **Board** and **Goals** switch and one button. On the Board it is **+ New task**. On Goals it is **+ Goal**.

**On the Board**

A row of filters sits above the columns:

- **Find a task** searches task numbers, titles and agent names.
- **Everyone** narrows the board to one agent's tasks.
- **All goals and projects** narrows it to the tasks that serve one goal or project.
- **Show cancelled** adds a list of cancelled tasks below the board.

There are five columns, each with a count:

- **To do**: waiting their turn. Each card shows its place in its agent's queue, such as "Next" or "3rd in queue".
- **Working**: being done now.
- **Needs you**: the agent is waiting for your answer or approval, or the task is stuck.
- **To review**: finished. Accept it or send it back.
- **Done**: tasks finished in the last two weeks.

Each card shows the task number, title, priority (unless Normal) and agent, plus its project or goal, open sub-tasks, comments and cost when it has them.

**On Goals**

Your mission is at the top. Below it are goal cards, grouped under "The whole organization" and each group on your chart. A goal card shows:

- a progress bar toward the target, with a **Now at** box to update the number
- the due date, in red once it is overdue
- how many of its tasks are done
- its projects, each with its lead and task count, and **Tasks**, **+ Task** and **Edit** buttons

## Key ideas

**Each agent has its own queue.** An agent works on one task at a time. When it is free, it starts its next task: urgent first, then high, normal and low. Among tasks of the same priority, the oldest goes first. Up to six tasks can run at once across the whole organization. Raising a task's priority moves it up its agent's queue. It never interrupts a task that has already started.

**What each status means.**
- **To do**: in the queue, not started.
- **Working**: the agent is on it. A task also stays here while it waits for sub-tasks, and its note says something like "Waiting on 2 sub-tasks".
- **Needs you**: the agent wants approval for a step, or asked you something before starting. The card says which.
- **Stuck**: shown in the Needs you column. The task failed, reached its budget, was stopped, did not pass its check, or could not start. The reason is on the card.
- **To review**: the agent has finished and written a report.
- **Done**: you accepted it. Tasks agents hand to each other also go straight to Done when they finish, because their report goes back to the agent who asked for it.

**Review and send back.** Work you give always comes back to you in **To review**. **Accept** marks it done. Writing a comment and pressing **Send back with this comment** wakes the agent. It reads your comment, carries on with the same task and reports again. You can also comment on a task that is already Done. It goes back to work.

**Comments reach the agent at the right moment.** Before a task starts, comments become part of its instructions. While it is working, a comment waits until the agent's current step ends.

**Sub-tasks and hand-offs.** You can split a task into sub-tasks yourself. Agents do the same thing: a lead hands parts of a job to the people who report to it, or asks another department for help. Each hand-off becomes a sub-task in the receiving agent's queue and waits its turn like any other task. When it finishes, its report goes to the agent who handed it on, not to you. The parent task waits until all its sub-tasks are back, then puts the results together. A task and everything handed on from it share one budget, the budget per task set in Settings.

**Goals, projects and tasks.** A goal is something you can measure, with a date, such as "40 wholesale customers by March". A project is a piece of work that serves a goal, and it can have a lead. A task can belong to a project (and so serve its goal), or serve a goal directly. When a task starts, its agent is told which goal and project it serves, with the goal's current progress, and to keep the work aimed at that. Every agent is also told your active goals (the organization's, and its own group's) and asked to put what moves them forward first.

**Every task has exits, and leaves a note when it stops.** Besides its budget, a task stops on its own when it has worked for 30 minutes (5 for a question to a colleague), when it tries the very same step a third time, or when every step fails four times in a row. Each of these means it is stuck or going round in circles, so it stops before wasting more money. Agents also cannot send work or questions back up their own chain (for example, a bookkeeper asking the head of finance who gave it the work), because that would go round in circles; they say in their report what they need instead.

**The task board.** Every task, together with the parts handed on from it, has one shared **Task board**. The agents working on it post short notes there as they go: a finding the others should know, a decision and why, a question, or a blocker. Every agent on the task sees the latest notes when it starts, and can read and post while it works, so two agents do not research the same thing and a decision reaches everyone at once. You can read the board on the task's page, and post to it yourself.

**Hand-over notes.** Whenever a task stops before finishing (its budget, a time limit, getting stuck, an error, or you stopping it), its agent leaves a **Hand-over note** on the task: why it stopped, what it did last, which files and pages it changed, what it last said, and where to pick up. You see it in the task's comments and on its page. **Try again** hands the note back to the agent, so it carries on from there instead of starting over.

## How to…

**Create a task**
1. On the Board, click **+ New task**.
2. Under **Task**, write a short title. Under **Details**, say what good looks like.
3. Choose who does it under **Assign to**, and set the **Priority**.
4. Optionally choose a goal or project under **Serves**.
5. Click **Create task**. It joins the agent's queue, and its panel opens.

**Open a task**
1. Click any card. A panel opens on the right with its status, buttons, details, the agent's report, sub-tasks and comments.
2. Click **✕** to close it.

**Set or change the priority**
1. Open the task.
2. Choose **Urgent**, **High**, **Normal** or **Low** under **Priority**. Once a task is done or cancelled, you can't change it.

**Reassign a task**
1. Open a task that is still in **To do**.
2. Pick another agent under **Assigned to**.

**Give a stopped task to someone else**
1. Open a task that is **Stuck** (it stopped at its budget, got stuck, failed, or you stopped it).
2. Choose an agent from **Give it to someone else…** and confirm.
3. The new agent starts a fresh run. Its brief includes the **Hand-over note** the last agent left, so it picks up from there instead of starting over. The first agent's run stays in the task's history.

A task that is still working must be stopped first, and a finished task cannot be moved: create a new task instead.

**Use the task board**
1. Open a task's page (from **Work in progress** or the task's panel) and open **Task board**.
2. Read what the agents on the task have posted: findings, decisions, questions, blockers and hand-overs. It opens by itself when one has a question or a blocker.
3. To tell every agent on the task something at once, write a note, choose its kind and press **Post**. They see it the next time they read the board or start.

**Rename a task or link it to a project or goal**
1. Open the task and edit the title at the top. It saves when you click away.
2. Choose a **Project**, or choose a **Goal** if it has no project. A task in a project serves that project's goal.

**Filter the board**
1. Use **Find a task**, **Everyone** or **All goals and projects** above the columns.

**Comment on a task**
1. Open the task and write in the box at the bottom.
2. Click **Comment**, or press ⌘↵ (Ctrl+Enter on Windows).

**Accept or send back finished work**
1. Open a task in **To review** and read **Its report**.
2. Click **Accept** if you are happy with it.
3. Otherwise write what should change and click **Send back with this comment**.

**Answer a task that needs you**
1. Open the task. If it is waiting for you, click **Answer it**.
2. On the task's own page, approve the step or reply in the conversation.

**Retry a stuck task**
1. Open the task and read the reason in the red banner.
2. Fix the cause if you can, for example by raising a budget.
3. Click **Try again**. A task that never started goes back in the queue. One that stopped carries on from where it left off.

**Cancel a task**
1. Open the task and click **Cancel task** at the bottom.
2. Confirm. Any open sub-tasks are cancelled too, and work done so far is kept.

**Add a sub-task**
1. Open the parent task and click **+ Sub-task**.
2. Fill in the form as for a new task. It starts with the parent's agent and priority, and serves the same goal or project unless you choose another.

**Set a goal**
1. Switch to **Goals** and click **+ Goal**.
2. Write the **Goal**. Add a **Target**, where it is **Now at** and what it is **Counted in** (for example "customers").
3. Pick a date under **By**, and under **For** choose the whole organization or one group.
4. Click **Set the goal**.

**Update a goal's progress**
1. On the goal card, type the new number in **Now at**. It saves when you click away.

**Mark a goal achieved, drop it or remove it**
1. Click **Mark achieved** on the card.
2. To drop or remove it, click **Edit**, then **Drop this goal** or **Remove**. Removing keeps its projects and tasks, without the goal.
3. Tick **Show achieved and dropped goals** to see them again. Click **Make it active again** to bring one back.

**Start a project**
1. On a goal card, click **+ Project**.
2. Name it, say what it covers under **About**, and choose who it is **Led by**.
3. Click **Start the project**. Use **Edit** later to change its status to **Paused** or **Done**.

**Add a task to a goal or project**
1. Click **+ Task** on the goal card or project row. The form opens with that goal or project chosen, and with the project's lead as the agent.

**See a goal's or project's tasks**
1. Click **See its tasks** on a goal, or **Tasks** on a project. The Board opens, filtered to them.

## Tips

- Say what a good result looks like, so the agent needn't ask.
- Use **Urgent** sparingly. If everything is urgent, nothing goes first.
- Give projects a lead. When you add a task from a project, it goes to the lead by default.
- Keep each goal's **Now at** number current. Agents see the progress, so a stale number misleads them.
- The Done column only shows the last two weeks. Older finished work is still on **Work in progress** under **Completed work**, and in your Inbox.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Before this task starts"**: the app found something missing before spending anything, such as an AI model or a key. Use the button shown to fix it. If **Start anyway** appears, the task can run without it.
- **"It is working now. Stop it first, then give it to someone else."**: press **Stop** on the task's page, wait until it shows its hand-over note, then choose **Give it to someone else…**.
- **"This task finished and is waiting for your review."**: a finished task is not moved. Send it back with a comment, or create a new task for someone else.
- **"This task's board is full"**: a task's board keeps up to 60 notes; the agents put the rest in their reports.
- **"Its agent is no longer on your team."**: the agent was removed. Reassign the task, then click **Try again**.
- **"It reached its budget."** or a monthly budget message: raise the budget (per task in Settings, per agent on the agent's page, or per group on the Organization page), then click **Try again**.
- **"Stopped: it tried the same step … a third time"** or **"Stopped: every step failed 4 times in a row"**: the agent is stuck. Read its **Hand-over note** and the last errors, add a comment with a hint (for example where the file really is), then click **Try again**.
- **"Stopped at its time limit"**: the task ran for over 30 minutes. Click **Try again** to let it carry on from its hand-over note, or split the task into smaller ones.
- **"… is further up this same task … would go round in circles"**: an agent tried to send work back to the agent that gave it the work. It finishes its part and says in its report what it needs; nothing to fix.
- **"Its result did not pass its check."**: open the work to see which check failed.
- **"The lead must be a position on the chart."**: add that agent to your Organization chart first.
