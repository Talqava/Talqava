# Words Talqava uses

One word for each thing, the same in every screen and guide.

## The work

- **Task**: one piece of work you give to an agent or a team. It has an owner, a status, a cost and a result.
- **Plan**: what an agent says it will do before it starts, for you to approve.
- **Project**: a folder for one body of work, holding its tasks and chats.
- **Routine**: work that repeats by itself, such as a report every Monday.
- **Goal**: an outcome several tasks work towards.
- **Inbox**: everything that needs you: plans, questions, finished work, changes to accept, reminders.

## The team

- **Agent**: an AI worker with a role, such as Bookkeeper. Agents are named by their job.
- **The assistant**: the agent you talk to in Chat. It works for you directly, outside the chart.
- **Position**: a place on the organization chart that an agent fills.
- **Group**: a department, such as Finance, with a lead.
- **Lead**: the agent that runs a group, splits work and reports to you.
- **Power**: something an agent is allowed to do, such as use the web or change files.
- **Marketplace**: ready-made agents, teams, templates, page packs and connectors.

## What you make and know

- **Office**: documents, sheets, slides and notes you and your agents work on.
- **Document**: writing, such as a letter or proposal. **Sheet**: numbers that calculate. **Slides**: a presentation. **Note**: quick and private until you share it.
- **Workspace page**: how your business works, written down: procedures, price lists, decisions. Agents follow these.
- **Table**: records with typed columns on a page, such as a customer list.
- **Library**: your files, in folders, that agents may read.
- **Memory**: short facts agents should always remember.
- **Skill**: a procedure an agent wrote down for itself.
- **Suggestion**: a change an agent wants to make to something of yours. It waits until you accept or decline.

## Staying in control

- **Approval**: your yes or no to a step an agent wants to take.
- **Budget**: the most an agent, a task or the whole team may spend.
- **Guardrail**: a rule for every agent that no agent's own settings can loosen.
- **Sensitivity level**: how sensitive an item is (Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted), which decides which AI services may receive it.
- **Protected**: an item agents can never change directly, only suggest changes to.
- **Private**: an item no agent can find or read.
- **Role** (for people): Owner, Administrator, Manager, Member or Auditor; what a person may do.
- **Second step**: a code from your phone, needed as well as your password.
- **Audit log**: the record of everything people and agents did.

## Connecting things

- **AI model**: the service your agents think with. **Backup model**: one that takes over if the first is down.
- **Connector**: a link to another service, such as Stripe, that gives agents tools for it. (Technically an MCP server.)
- **Key**: a secret from another service that lets Talqava use your account there.
- **Single sign-on**: signing in with your company account.

## Older words you may still see

- **Bot** meant agent. **Quest** meant task. **Schedule** meant routine. **Work in progress** was the name of the Agents page.
