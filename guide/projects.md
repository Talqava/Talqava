# Projects

A project is one folder for a body of work, such as onboarding a client, a launch or a month-end close. It holds the work's tasks, its chats and its own memory, its lead, the goal it serves, what "done" looks like and its due day. Open a project to see where the work stands and what needs you.

## What you'll see

- **Projects** in the menu, under **Work**.
- **A card for each project**, grouped by **Active**, **Planning**, **Paused** and **Done** (Done folds away). A card shows the name, how it is doing (**On track**, **At risk** or **Off track**), its lead and goal, tasks done out of the total, how many chats it has, its due day, and how many things need you.
- **Filters** above the cards: **Needs me**, **Any goal** and **Any lead**.
- **+ New project** at the top right.
- **A project's page**, when you click a card: its name, lead, goal and due day, a status menu, **Edit** and **Remove**, and three parts: **Overview**, **Tasks** and **Chats**.

## Key ideas

**One meaning of "project".** A project used to mean two things: a group of tasks under a goal, and a folder of chats with its own memory. They are now the same thing. Every chat folder you had is a project, with its chats and memory as they were, and every project can hold chats.

**A project's chats keep their own memory.** What an agent learns in a project's chats is used only in that project's chats, together with your General memory. This keeps one client's details out of another's work. See [Memory](memory.md).

**How it is doing is worked out, not typed.** A project is **Off track** when it is past its due day or one of its tasks has waited more than a day. It is **At risk** when a task has stopped moving, or when most of its time is used and under 60% of its tasks are done. Otherwise it is **On track**. Hover over the word to see why. Only active projects are rated.

**Tasks do not have to be in a project.** Quick one-off tasks can stay outside. "No goal or project" is a normal choice.

**Removing a project keeps its tasks.** They stay on the board without a project. Its chats move to General, and the project's own memory is deleted.

## How to…

### Start a project

1. Open **Projects** and press **+ New project**.
2. Type a name. Add what it covers and, under **Done when**, what finished looks like in your own words.
3. Choose a **Due** day, a **Goal** it serves and who it is **Led by**, if you wish. Leave **Status** as **Active**, or choose **Planning** if work has not started.
4. Press **Start the project**. Its page opens.

### Add a task to a project

1. Open the project and choose **Tasks**.
2. Press **+ Task**. The form opens with the project chosen and its lead as the agent.
3. To move an existing task into a project, open the task on the **Tasks** page and choose the project under **Project**.

### Chat inside a project

1. Open the project and choose **Chats**.
2. Press **+ Chat in this project**. The chat opens in the project, with the project's memory.
3. Earlier chats are listed here. Click one to open it.

You can also pick the project at the top of the chat list on the **Chat** page.

### Keep a project's files with it

1. Open the project and go to **Files**.
2. Press **+ Document**, **+ Sheet**, **+ Slides** or **+ Note** to start a file in the project.
3. To add a file you already have, open it and choose the project under **Project** in its side panel.

Agents working on the project's tasks are told which files belong to it, so they start from them.

### See what needs you

1. On the **Projects** page, tick **Needs me** to see only projects with something waiting on you.
2. Open one. **Needs you** on the **Overview** lists the tasks to review, answer or unblock. Click a task to open it.

### Pause, finish or remove a project

1. Open the project and choose **Paused** or **Done** in the status menu. Its tasks are not changed by this; pause or finish them on the **Tasks** page.
2. To remove it, press **Remove** and confirm.

## Tips

- Give each client or launch its own project, so its chats, memory and tasks stay together.
- Write **Done when** as something you could check, such as "Ten shops stock the new line".
- Give a project a lead. New tasks you add from the project go to the lead by default.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Give the project a name."** Type a name before saving.
- **"The date is a day, like 2027-03-01."** Pick the due day from the calendar.
- **"There is no such project."** It was removed. Go back to **Projects**.
- **A project's name looks shorter in the chat list.** Names there are limited to 40 characters.
