# Marketplace

The Marketplace is where you find ready-made agents and teams for every part of your business: sales, marketing, finance, operations and more. Every agent is free, and you can read exactly what it is told to do before you add it. Once added, the agent is your own copy, and it only changes when you change it.

## What you'll see

- **Marketplace** at the top, with a row of category buttons: **All**, one button per department (such as Sales, Finance or Engineering), and **On my team**.
- A search box: **Search by agent name or what you need done**.
- **Featured**: a few agents picked out for a quick start (shown when no category or search is chosen).
- **Teams**: ready-made groups, each a lead and the agents that report to it. A team card shows how many agents it has, who the lead is, and a button such as **Add all 4**.
- One section per department, with a card for each agent. A card shows the agent's name, its job title, a one-line summary, and either **Free · asks before changes** or **Free · runs everything**. Agents you already have show **✓ On your team** and an **Edit** button instead of **Add**.

Click any agent card to open its own page. There you will see:

- Its name, job title, department and who it reports to.
- An **At a glance** panel: **Price**, **Can use** (for example web search, memory, your files), **Cannot use**, **Asks first**, **Model** and **Limit per job**.
- **Instructions**, shown in full with the note **Shown in full · nothing hidden**, and a **Copy** button.
- **✓ Will do** and **✕ Will not do** lists.
- For some roles, what the agent is **Measured on**, what it **Decides itself**, and what it **Must escalate** to you or its manager.

## Key ideas

- **Nothing is hidden.** You see every word an agent is told before you add it. This lets you decide if you trust it with your business.
- **Your copy is yours.** Adding an agent saves a copy on your computer, and remembers which version it was. A newer version is offered, never forced: you choose **Update** or **Keep mine**, and an update can be undone. Its name, look, model and budget always stay as you set them.
- **Every listing is checked and labelled.** Each one shows its version, who made it (**Made by Talqava**, **Verified** or **Community**) and its licence. Before a listing is offered, Talqava checks it automatically: it must ask before changing anything, only engineering roles may run commands, and its instructions must not try to override its rules or hide what it does.
- **Agents arrive in the right place.** An added agent joins a group named after its department, and takes its place on your org chart under the role it normally reports to, if you have that role.
- **"Asks first" protects you.** An agent marked **asks before changes** stops and asks you before running a command, changing a file or writing to another service. One marked **runs everything** does not. You can change this on the agent's page after you add it.
- **Teams save time.** Adding a team adds the lead and everyone below it, with their reporting lines already drawn. Agents you already have keep their place.

## How to…

### Start from a company template

1. Under **Company templates**, find your kind of business: **Professional services firm**, **Restaurant or café**, **Online shop** or **Nonprofit**.
2. Press **Preview and install**. The preview lists the positions it adds to your chart, the page packs, and the routines.
3. Press **Install**. Nothing you already have is removed or moved, and nothing is spent. Its routines arrive **paused**: switch on the ones you want under **Routines**.
4. **Undo**, in the note at the bottom or on the template's card, puts your chart back as it was, moves its pages to Deleted and removes its routines. The agents it added stay on your team, off the chart.

### Install and run a playbook

A playbook is one known process: **Month-end close**, **Hiring** or **Client onboarding**.
1. Press **Preview and install**. It adds the procedure as a page in your Workspace, and for the month-end close a paused monthly routine for your Finance lead.
2. Press **Run it now** on its card whenever you want it done. The work goes to the lead of the group that owns it, who sends you a plan to approve before anything starts.
3. If your chart has no such group yet, Talqava says which one to add.

### Connect a service your business already uses

1. Scroll to **Connectors**: Stripe, Airtable, monday.com, Intercom, Linear and GitHub.
2. Press **See what it can do** to read what it can read and change, then paste a key you make in that service and press **Check the key and connect**.
3. **Not available yet** says which services cannot be connected yet, and why.

See [Integrations](integrations.md) for the details.

### Add a page pack to your Workspace

1. Scroll to **Page packs**: ready-made pages such as an **SOP library**, a **Price list** and handbooks.
2. Press **Preview and add**. The preview lists the pages, who will see them, and which start protected.
3. Press **Add these pages**. The pack opens in your Workspace. **Undo** in the note at the bottom takes it back; later, delete the pack's top page (it stays in Deleted for 30 days).

### Find an agent for a job

1. Open **Marketplace** in the left menu.
2. Type what you need in the search box, for example "invoices" or "social posts". Or press a department button.
3. Press **On my team** to see only the Marketplace agents you have already added.

### Check an agent before you add it

1. Click the agent's card.
2. Read **At a glance**, especially **Can use**, **Cannot use** and **Asks first**.
3. Read the **Instructions** and the **Will do** and **Will not do** lists.
4. Press **← Back to marketplace** to keep looking.

### Add one agent

1. On the agent's card, press **Add**. Or open its page and press **Add to my team · Free**.
2. A preview opens. Nothing has changed yet. It shows who the agent reports to, what it can use, that it asks before changing anything, its spending limit per task, and what it will never do.
3. Press **Add to my team**, or **Cancel**. The agent joins your organization, and its page shows **✓ On your team**.

### Add a whole team

1. Find the team under **Teams**.
2. Press **Add all** (or **Add the other** if you already have some of its agents).
3. A preview lists every agent: who each reports to, what each can use and may cost a task, and which you already have (those stay as they are). It also gives the most one task from every new agent would cost. Nothing has changed yet.
4. Press **Add**, or **Cancel**. Talqava takes you to your **Organization** page so you can see where the team landed.

### Update an agent, or undo an update

1. When agents on your team have a newer version, a note at the top of the Marketplace says so. Press **Show them**.
2. Open an agent marked **Update … available**. Its page says which version you have, and lists what changed.
3. Press **Update to …**, or **Keep mine** to leave your copy as it is. If you had changed its instructions, Talqava asks before replacing them.
4. Changed your mind? Press **Undo update** on its page to put the previous version back.

### Use an agent you have added

On its Marketplace page you can:

1. Press **Chat with** the agent to talk to it straight away.
2. Press **Assign a task to the** agent to go to Work in progress with it chosen.
3. Press **Edit your copy** to change its name, look, instructions, model, approvals or budget.

## Tips

- Start small. Add one or two agents for your most pressing work, try a task, then grow.
- Read the **Will not do** list. It tells you where you still need a person.
- If no agent matches, you can create your own from the **Work in progress** page with **+ Create an agent**.
- Use **Copy** on the instructions if you want to start your own agent from a ready-made one.

## If something goes wrong

- **"No agent matches that."** Try other words, or clear the search and pick a department.
- **"You have not added a marketplace agent yet."** You pressed **On my team** before adding anyone. Press **All** and add an agent.
- **A red box at the top listing a file.** One Marketplace listing could not be read, so it is left out. The rest work as normal.
- **A team button is greyed out and says ✓ On your chart.** You already have every agent in that team.
